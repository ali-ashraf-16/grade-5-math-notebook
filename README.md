Style: Premium dark-mode educational presentation slide (16:9), styled as a handcrafted Grade 5 math notebook page mounted in a dark walnut wood frame (70px) against a dark slate wall. 

Base Design: Dark charcoal paper (#1E1E1E), faint-gray grid, realistic drop shadow, matte silver binder clips at the top, and subtle gray masking tape at the bottom corners.

Typography & Colors: Handwritten classroom fonts. Pastel Orange (#FF8C32) for titles & key formula boxes; Sky Blue (#38BDF8) for headings; Off-White (#F5F5F7) for body text; Neon Red (#FF4D4D) / Lime Green (#39FF14) for math numerators, denominators, and highlights.

- Slide 1 (Title Slide): Contains the Lesson Title centered in large Pastel Orange, with the Lesson Number (e.g., Lesson 3) in Sky Blue, and a small signature reading "Prepared by Mr. Ali Ashraf" in elegant handwritten Off-White at the bottom center.

Pedagogical Structure (One Idea Per Slide):
- Strictly one single mathematical concept or idea per slide.
- Content Flow Sequence: 
1. Concept Explanation Slide (Heading + Brief text explanation).
2. Solved Example Slide (Clear step-by-step resolved equation or model).
3. Unsolved Example Slide (An identical-style practice problem for the audience to work on, with NO answer or solution displayed).

Quiz Slide Variant (10-Question Quiz):
- Designed as a 10-slide sequence.
- Strictly one quiz question per slide. 
- Clean, focused layout centered around the question text/equation. 
- Absolutely NO answers, solutions, or hints displayed on the slides.

Details: Small hand-drawn math doodles on margins only. No modern icons. Soft natural side lighting with realistic shadows. High contrast and premium photographic quality.
